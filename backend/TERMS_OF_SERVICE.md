Report content that violates these terms using the in-app report option — routes, photos, and comments that collect enough reports are automatically hidden pending review, and our moderators can review, hide, remove, or ban accounts directly for real violations. Don't:

## SOS — read this before you rely on it

NomadCompass's SOS screen opens a pre-filled text message addressed to your own emergency contacts with your last known GPS position, using your device's own Messages app. **That is the entire mechanism.** It is not a satellite beacon, not a monitored distress signal, and nothing is dispatched automatically — a message only reaches anyone if you have phone signal, the Messages app actually sends it, and a contact you added actually sees it and chooses to act. NomadCompass has no relationship with any emergency dispatch service and cannot guarantee a message is ever delivered or seen.

**NomadCompass is not a substitute for calling local emergency services, and not a substitute for a dedicated satellite messenger (e.g. a Garmin inReach or similar) on any trip beyond cell coverage.** You use SOS, the route guidance, the Field Guide, and every other feature of this app entirely at your own risk. We are not liable for any injury, loss, or damage arising from reliance on anything in the app, including a failed or undelivered SOS message.

## Subscriptions

NomadCompass Pro is an auto-renewing subscription sold and billed entirely through the App Store, under Apple's own terms. Price, billing cycle, renewal, and cancellation are all managed in your Apple ID account settings (Settings → your name → Subscriptions), not by us — we never see or store your payment details. Pro currently unlocks the full leg-by-leg itinerary on every route; what it includes may change, and we'll reflect that honestly in the app and on the paywall, not retroactively reinterpret what you already paid for.

## Account deletion

You can delete your account at any time from Settings → Delete account. This is permanent: it removes your profile and, where you're the sole owner, your routes, photos, and comments. It cannot be undone and we cannot recover it for you afterward.

## Our right to suspend or remove content

We can hide or remove content, or suspend or ban an account, for a real violation of these terms — most routes/photos/comments through the report-and-auto-hide system above, more serious or repeated violations through direct admin review. We'll always prefer hiding/removing the specific content over banning an account where that's enough to address the problem.

Route distance, elevation, and requirement data comes from other users — verify anything safety-critical yourself before you rely on it.
