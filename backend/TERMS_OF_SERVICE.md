## Hazard reports are community information

Hazard reports (fallen trees, broken bridges, deep fords and similar) are submitted by other users and may be wrong, out of date or missing. The absence of a report does not mean a route is safe. Always judge conditions yourself.

## Weather information

The Meteo feature shows forecast data from third parties (Open-Meteo) and radar imagery (RainViewer). Forecasts are uncertain, radar shows recent past precipitation, not a prediction, and risk colours and the "best window" are rough guidance based on simple rules, not a safety guarantee. Check official forecasts and use your own judgement before you set out.

## SOS: read this before you rely on it

Nomad's SOS screen opens a pre-filled text message addressed to your own emergency contacts with your last known GPS position, using your device's Messages app. **That is the entire mechanism.** It is not a satellite beacon, not a monitored distress signal, and nothing is dispatched automatically. A message reaches anyone only if you have phone signal, Messages actually sends it, and a contact sees it and chooses to act. Nomad has no relationship with any emergency dispatch service and cannot guarantee a message is ever delivered or seen.

**Nomad is not a substitute for calling local emergency services, or for a dedicated satellite messenger (for example Garmin inReach) on any trip beyond cell coverage.** You use SOS, route guidance, weather, hazard reports, the Field Guide and every other feature entirely at your own risk. We are not liable for any injury, loss or damage arising from reliance on anything in the app, including a failed or undelivered SOS message.
